# Dashboard

The dashboard is the starting point after sign-in. Its greeting and cards
summarize the clinic's work. Cards depend on installed modules and your role,
so two team members may see different sections.

## Before you sign in

When nobody is signed in, the same `/` address shows the public Dentum landing
page. Choose **Sign in** to continue. A fresh installation opens the initial
setup flow; after signing in, Dentum returns you to the dashboard.

## Find a task

1. Use the main navigation to open patients, appointments, budgets, reports,
   and other areas available to your account.
2. Open a dashboard card to continue into the related record or workflow.
3. If the dashboard is empty, complete the clinic setup and add the first
   patients or appointments. The welcome panel appears until modules provide
   dashboard content.

## When something is missing

Ask an administrator to check that the relevant module is installed and that
your account has permission to view its data. Dashboard cards are hidden when
the module or permission is unavailable.
